Today we will see how to consume a SOAP service. What have we done till now? We have already seen how to consume a third-party REST service, and we have created a REST service. But we will not create a SOAP service, because 99% of the time we build all the latest applications on REST. Since we build on REST, there is little scope to build SOAP services.

Next question: since we are not building SOAP services, why should we consume them? If we look at older applications, they have already built SOAP services. How do they expose their services to us? They are not changing them — they keep them as they are and expose a SOAP service to us. Then we have to consume that SOAP service. Such situations are rare, but we should still know about it. We have a third-party service: how should we call it from our REST service, how to send the input, and how to manipulate the output — a clear and easy use case for all of us.
