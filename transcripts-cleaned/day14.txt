Now a small thing. Remember, we'd put the DB connection details — username, password, all of it — in the property file. Can we keep it like that? No. We have to encrypt it. What's encryption? We've seen it. Two systems — say my WhatsApp and my friend Ramesh's WhatsApp. I send "Hi Ramesh, how are you?". If it went as is, it'd go exactly like that. But WhatsApp conversations are end-to-end encrypted. Encrypting means converting from a readable format — which anyone who hacks it can read easily: "Hi Ramesh", sent by Mahesh — to an unreadable format. Converting readable to unreadable is encryption. [drawing: "Encryption — readable format → algorithm + key → unreadable format; E → RF→URF; D → URF→RF".]
